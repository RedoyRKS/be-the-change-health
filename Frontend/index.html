Our Approach

At Be the Change Health & Wellness Center, we focus on the root cause.

The human body consists of complex interconnected body systems, each with its unique functions and dependencies. They all rely on each other to maintain overall health and functionality. Through integrative medicine and naturopathic medicine, we provide a holistic approach that addresses the root cause of your symptoms and considers these body systems in order to stimulate the body’s natural ability to heal itself from the inside out.

We help you get to the root cause of your health concerns using the latest advances in integrative medicine. Our proactive approach supports positive and lasting changes in your happiness and life.

What makes us different is that we practice personalized medicine. We consider your physical, mental and emotional needs, and may use specialty tests of the immune system, nervous system, digestive system and nutrigenetic analysis, and more.

  • Functional lab testing

    Identify underlying imbalances with personalized testing and clear next steps.

  • Integrative & naturopathic medicine

    Blend conventional insight with natural therapies to support lasting wellness.

  • Compassionate guidance

    Care for complex chronic needs with kindness, generosity, and compassion.
